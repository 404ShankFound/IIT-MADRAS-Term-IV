MOCK A: HEALTH REPORTS + SUM + SEARCH
Patients upload reports encrypted with AES-256-CBC. Sign SHA-512 with ElGamal.
Assigned doctors decrypt and verify. Administrators see hashes/timestamps only.
Encrypt consultation charges with Paillier and total without reading each fee.
Search encrypted records by diagnosis keyword. Revoke a patient's upload access.

Start: scenarios/02_MEDISECURE.py or filter AES_CBC/SHA512/ELGAMAL/PAILLIER/SSE.
Rename users/labels. Upload charges 15 and 25; expected total 40. Query a shared
keyword; expect both records for auditor, only own record for each patient.
Demonstrate wrong user and auditor plaintext denial; modify data and reject it.

MOCK B: CLOUD ORDERS + PRODUCT + SEARCH
Buyers submit orders to a named supplier. RSA protects a fresh AES session key.
Use SHA256 and Schnorr signatures. Supplier approves/rejects each order.
Use ElGamal multiplicative HE for two numeric factors. Auditor searches orders
by product keyword and sees only hashes, time and verification metadata.

Start: RSA_HYBRID/SHA256/SCHNORR/ELGAMAL/SSE combination.
For 7 and 3 expected product 21 (not 10). Reject one order and recompute the
aggregate. Change recipient field after signing and demonstrate rejection.

BONUS 30-MIN DRILL

BE ABLE TO ANSWER THESE VIVA QUESTIONS
- Why does Paillier multiply ciphertexts to add plaintexts?
- Why is RSA OAEP unsuitable for multiplicative HE?
- Can ElGamal directly compare two blood pressure readings? No; explain why.
- What is signed, and which key verifies it?
- Does an unkeyed hash alone authenticate a sender? No.
- Why is H(plaintext) different from H(ciphertext)?
- Why does CBC need padding and a fresh IV?
- What does SSE reveal even when document text is encrypted?
- What is the difference between 3-of-3 partial decryption and reconstructing
  a shared private key with Shamir?
- Why does adding candidate IDs not count votes per candidate?
- Can a server holding the AES decryption key read a ballot? Yes.
- Does zero observed collisions in 100 random inputs prove hash security? No.
